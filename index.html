Questions

What does mead taste like?

Closer to wine than to honey. Our dry juniper is crisp like a Riesling; the buckwheat is deep and malty, close to a tawny port. Only the linden is properly sweet.

Do you ship outside Lithuania?

Yes, to every EU country with an age check on delivery. Most parcels arrive in four to six working days; delivery is €9.90, free in Lithuania over €60.

How long does an opened bottle keep?

Still meads keep for three to four weeks in the fridge with the swing top closed. Sparkling is best within two days.

Can we book the granary for a private event?

For up to 28 guests, Monday or Tuesday evenings. Write to us with a date and we will send a menu.
